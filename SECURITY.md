## 🛡️ Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to us as soon as possible. We will investigate all reports and do our best to fix the issue promptly.

### How to Report

-   **Email:** Send a detailed email to [security@codesphereug.com](mailto:security@codesphereug.com).
-   **Include:**
    -   A clear description of the vulnerability.
    -   Steps to reproduce the vulnerability.
    -   Any proof-of-concept code.

### What to Expect

-   We will acknowledge your report within 48 hours.
-   We will provide a timeline for a fix.
-   We will notify you when the vulnerability is patched.

---

## ❌ Scope

Please do not report vulnerabilities in third-party services. Only report vulnerabilities in our own systems.

---

## 📜 Responsible Disclosure

We request that you do not publicly disclose the vulnerability until we have had a chance to address it. We will work with you to ensure that the issue is resolved and that you receive credit for your discovery.

## 📞 Contact

For any security-related questions, please contact us at [security@codesphereug.com](mailto:security@codesphereug.com).
